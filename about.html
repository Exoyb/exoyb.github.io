// why security?

Why Cybersecurity?

My interest in security started when I was younger through online gaming. Getting kicked offline by DDoS attacks made me curious about what was actually happening and why. That curiosity opened the door to networking, malware, spoofing, software behaviour and how systems can be attacked or disrupted.

I never developed that interest into a career at the time, but the mindset never really disappeared. Even in retail and support roles, I naturally leaned toward the same questions: what happened, how did it happen, how do I stop it happening again, and what is the real root cause behind the symptom?

Why did it happen? → How did it happen? → How do I prevent it? → What does the evidence actually say?

Security Operations feels like the right next step because it combines investigation, technical reasoning, pattern recognition and practical problem-solving in a way that feels genuinely natural.
