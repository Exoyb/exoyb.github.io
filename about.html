# Why Security?

My interest in cybersecurity started years before I thought about it as a career. Online gaming gave me the first nudge: I got hit by a DDoS attack and, naturally, “my internet stopped working” wasn’t a satisfying enough answer.

I wanted to know what had happened, how someone had done it and what I could do to prevent it. That rabbit hole led me into networking, how denial-of-service attacks work, malware, MAC address spoofing and other attack techniques. I learned enough to understand how things could be done — and also that doing them back would be both childish and, rather importantly, illegal.

Retail became my career for a long time, so I never developed that interest as far as I wanted to. But the mindset never really disappeared. Hardware repair and technical support kept reinforcing the exact same habit: don’t stop at the symptom; understand the cause.

Why did I get kicked offline? → How did it happen? → How do I prevent it? → Can I reproduce the behaviour safely? → What else is happening under the hood?

Cybersecurity feels like picking that curiosity back up properly — but this time with years of troubleshooting, communication and professional experience behind it.
