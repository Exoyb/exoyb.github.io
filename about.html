// why security?

Why Cybersecurity?

The interest started with online gaming. After being knocked offline by DDoS attacks, “the internet broke” wasn’t a satisfying enough answer.

I wanted to understand what had happened, how it worked and how to stop it happening again. That curiosity led me into networking, malware, spoofing, software behaviour and attack techniques.

What happened? → How? → Why? → How do I prevent it? → What does the evidence say?

Years later, hardware repair and software support have reinforced the exact same habit: don’t stop at the symptom. Find the cause. Security Operations feels like the natural place to take that mindset next.
