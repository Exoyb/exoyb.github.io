The bits people usually ask me

Open whatever matters to you. The page is designed to be skimmed, not read like a biography.

Why Cybersecurity?Gaming, DDoS attacks and refusing to accept “the internet broke” as an answer.

My interest started through online gaming. After being knocked offline by DDoS attacks, I wanted to understand what had actually happened: what the attack was doing, why it worked and what could be done to stop it.

That curiosity spread into networking, malware, spoofing, software behaviour and attack techniques. I kept coming back to the same questions: What happened? How? Why? What evidence proves it?

Why now?A deliberate move built on years of troubleshooting, not a sudden career reset.

I’ve spent years building the pieces without originally calling them security skills: diagnosing hardware, testing electronics, dealing with fraud risk, supporting software, handling permissions and authentication issues, documenting fixes and escalating complex problems.

Now I’m deliberately turning that experience toward security through structured study, hands-on labs and documented work employers can actually inspect.

Why Security Operations / SOC?Investigation, triage and evidence are the parts of technical work I naturally gravitate toward.

SOC work appeals to me because it turns curiosity into a process: something happened, evidence exists somewhere, and the job is to work out what matters, what caused it and what should happen next.

That maps well to how I already approach support: narrow noisy problems, reproduce behaviour where possible, separate symptoms from causes and hand over useful context when escalation is needed.

Signal → Evidence → Triage → Root cause → Action → Documentation

What do I bring from support?Technical diagnosis matters, but so do communication, ownership and knowing when to escalate.

Support has taught me to work with incomplete information, ask better questions, stay calm when someone is frustrated and keep ownership of a problem even when the eventual fix sits with another team.
