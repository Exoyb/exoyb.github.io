Botium Toys Security Audit

My first structured security audit project. I reviewed Botium Toys’ assets, existing security controls and compliance practices, identified gaps and looked at where the biggest risks sat.

I then completed a controls and compliance checklist and produced a prioritised set of recommendations for the IT Manager. The aim wasn’t just to point at what was missing, but to explain why it mattered and suggest realistic ways the business could improve it.

Security AuditRisk AssessmentNIST CSFPCI DSSGDPRSOC
$ cat scope.txt

What I assessed

The audit covered the organisation’s wider security programme, including employee devices, internal systems and networks, data storage, legacy systems and existing physical and technical controls.

$ cat findings.txt

What stood out

The biggest gaps included broad access to sensitive data, no encryption for cardholder information, no least privilege or separation of duties, no IDS, no backups or disaster recovery plan, and weak password controls.

$ cat response.txt

What I recommended

I prioritised the recommendations by risk and implementation effort, then suggested practical fixes around access control, encryption, permissions, backups, disaster recovery, passwords, intrusion detection, asset classification and legacy system monitoring.

// recommendations --priority

Priorities

CRITICAL

Access control and encryption. Sensitive customer and payment data needed protecting first.

HIGH

Least privilege, separation of duties, backups, disaster recovery and stronger password security.

MEDIUM

Password management, IDS, asset classification and regular legacy system monitoring.
